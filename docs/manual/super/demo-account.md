# Demo account

Each site has one shared demo address: **demo@ballotbox.us.com** on
ballotbox.us.com, and **demo@staging.pollsystem.pages.dev** on staging. While
the demo is on, anyone who types that address on **Sign in** is signed straight
in as a paid member and creator. They get no email, no code, and no payment
page. It's for showing BallotBox to a prospective customer.

## Starting a demo

1. Open **Super Admin**. The dashboard has a **Demo account** card.
2. Enter the **Zipcode** the demo should live in. It decides which polls and
   which midterm ballot are theirs, and which state it can create polls for.
3. Click **Start (12 hours)**.
4. Give the person the address. They go to **Sign in**, type it, and they're
   in. A notice across the top says it's the demo account and when it ends.

## While it's on

- **+12 hours** adds 12 more hours.
- **End now** ends it at once.
- Anyone who knows the address can sign in until it ends, so keep it on only
  around a demo.
- On ballotbox.us.com its votes count in real results until it ends, and its
  published polls are visible to real members.

## When it ends

The demo ends when its time runs out (checked every 5 minutes) or when you
click **End now**. Then:

- its votes and comments are deleted, and results are recounted without them;
- its polls are **archived and handed to you** (the super who started it). You
  can find them under Manage Polls;
- the account is deleted, and anyone still using it is signed out on their next
  click.

To use it again, click **Start**. You get a fresh, empty demo account.
