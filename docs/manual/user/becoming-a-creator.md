# Becoming a creator

Creators publish their own polls: elections, questionnaires and ballot measures.
To become one, you send a request saying **where** and **what kind** of polls
you'd like to create, and an admin reviews it.

You need an active membership to send a request. Creators keep paying for their
membership like every other member.

## Sending a request

1. On your home page, click **Request Creator access** (on the **Become a
   Creator** card).
2. Under **Geographic scope**, choose how wide an area you're asking for:
   - **Specific zipcodes**
   - **Whole county(ies)**
   - **Whole state(s)**
   - **Nationwide**

   Then pick the states, counties or zipcodes.
3. Under **Poll Types**, tick the kinds of poll you want to create: Election,
   Questionnaire, and/or Referendum/Ballot Measure.
4. Optionally, add a **Reason**: why you'd like to create polls in this area.
   It helps the admin decide.
5. Click **Submit Request**.

You'll get an email confirming the request.

## What happens next

- Your request goes to an admin who looks after that area. A nationwide request,
  or one that waits more than two days, can be picked up by any admin.
- When it's decided, you get an email: **"You are now a Creator!"** or **"Your
  creator request was not approved"**.
- Once approved, your home page shows **Creator dashboard** and **Start a new
  poll**, and **Creator** appears in the top bar.

## What your access covers

Your access is the area and poll types you were approved for. You can create
and publish polls only of those types, and only in that area. To widen it, click
**Request more creator access** on your Creator dashboard and send another
request.

An admin can also:

- revise a decision later, so access you were given can be withdrawn;
- turn off your access in the area they look after, and disable polls there.

## If your membership lapses

If your subscription ends, your account goes back to a basic, non-member account
and **you lose creator access**. To get it back, renew your membership and send
a new request.
