# Answering polls

To answer a poll you need to be signed in with an active membership (see
[Signing up](../viewer/signing-up.md)), and your profile needs a
phone and zipcode.

Find the poll with [Finding polls](../viewer/finding-polls.md), then click
**Vote →** on its row. When you submit, you're taken to the poll's results.

## Elections

Candidates are grouped by office (for example, Mayor). Depending on the
election, you either:

- **choose one candidate** per office, from a list of options or a dropdown, or
- **choose any number** of candidates per office, or
- answer **Yes** or **No** for every candidate.

Where candidates are listed with a button or box beside them, you can click the
candidate's name to select them. Make a choice for every office, then click
**Submit votes**.

Each candidate has an optional **comment** box.

### Ranked-choice races

Some races are counted by **ranked choice**, as in Alaska. Their names end in
"(ranked choice)". Instead of picking one candidate, you **rank** them: pick
**1st**, **2nd**, **3rd**… from the dropdown beside each name.

- Rank as many or as few as you like, but give at least a **1st** choice.
- Ranks can't skip: a 3rd choice needs a 2nd.
- Picking a rank someone else already has moves it to your new pick, and the
  other candidate becomes unranked.

When you review a past ballot, each ranked candidate shows **Your 1st choice**,
**Your 2nd choice**, and so on.

## Ballot measures

A ballot measure belongs to an election, and you vote on it **on the
election's page**: the election's open measures are listed under
**Ballot measures**, below the candidates. **Vote →** on a measure in Find
polls takes you there, scrolled to that measure.

Measures are **optional**, like on a real ballot: choose **Yes** or **No** on
the ones you want to vote on, and leave the others blank. Each has an optional
comment box. **Submit votes** sends your candidates and measures together.

## Questionnaires

Answer **Yes** or **No** to every question. Each question has an optional
comment box. Click **Submit responses** when you're done.

## Changing your answers

You can change your answers until the poll closes. When you open a poll you've
already answered, you'll see when you answered and a choice:

- **Yes, edit**: change your answers, then click **Update votes** (or **Update
  vote** / **Update responses**). Your new answers replace the old ones.
- **No, just review**: see your answers without changing them.

You can answer each poll once; updating replaces your earlier answers rather
than adding a second set.

## About your comments

Comments are **private** unless you tick **Make comment public (and
anonymous)** under the comment box. Private comments aren't shown in the
results, and the poll's creator doesn't see them.

A **public** comment is shown on the results page under that question,
candidate or measure, **without your name**. It follows the same privacy rules
as the results: nothing shows until 4 people have responded, and new comments
appear with the next step of 4.

Public comments can't contain certain words. When you submit, each one found is
highlighted in its comment box, and you're asked **"Can I replace *word* with
*other word*?"**:

- **Yes** replaces it, and the comment stays public.
- **No** keeps that comment **private**. Your answers are still submitted.

Untick the box later, and update your answers, to make a comment private again.

BallotBox's super administrators can see all comments, public or private,
when reviewing an account.

## Why can't I answer?

- **"This poll is no longer available for responses."** The poll has closed.
  You can still view its results.
- **"An active subscription is required to participate."** Your membership
  has lapsed: renew from your home page.
- **"Complete your profile."** Add your phone and zipcode.
- **"Submissions disabled by admin for this area."** An admin has turned this
  poll off where you live. Results stay visible.
- **"Poll submissions are temporarily disabled by an administrator."**
  Answering is paused for everyone; try again later.
- **"Please make a selection for…"** or **"Please answer…"**: a question or
  office still needs an answer.

## Your past ballots

**View past ballots** on your home page lists every ballot and questionnaire
you've answered, newest vote first, with the date of your vote. A midterm ballot
appears once, with its statewide races and measures. After **View**, two
columns say whether each poll is **Closed** (hover for its close date) and
**Enabled?**: **No** means an admin has switched it off in your area, or
everywhere.

Click **View** to see that ballot with your choices highlighted: **✓ Your vote**
on each candidate you picked, your Yes or No on each measure and question, and
your comments. It's read-only, and works after the poll has closed too. To
change a vote while the poll is open, open it from **Vote** instead.
