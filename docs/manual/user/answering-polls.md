# Answering polls

To answer a poll you need to be signed in with an active membership (see
[Signing up and signing in](../viewer/signing-up.md)), and your profile needs a
phone and zipcode.

Find the poll with [Finding polls](../viewer/finding-polls.md), then click
**Vote →** on its row. When you submit, you're taken to the poll's results.

## Elections

Candidates are grouped by office (for example, Mayor). Depending on the
election, you either:

- **choose one candidate** per office, from a list of options or a dropdown, or
- **choose any number** of candidates per office, or
- answer **Yes** or **No** for every candidate.

Where candidates are listed with a button or box beside them, you can click the
candidate's name to select them. Make a choice for every office, then click
**Submit votes**.

Each candidate has an optional **comment** box.

## Ballot measures

A ballot measure belongs to an election, shown as **Part of**. Choose **Yes**
or **No** under **Your vote**, add a comment if you like, and click **Submit
vote**.

## Questionnaires

Answer **Yes** or **No** to every question. Each question has an optional
comment box. Click **Submit responses** when you're done.

## Changing your answers

You can change your answers until the poll closes. When you open a poll you've
already answered, you'll see when you answered and a choice:

- **Yes, edit**: change your answers, then click **Update votes** (or **Update
  vote** / **Update responses**). Your new answers replace the old ones.
- **No, just review**: see your answers without changing them.

You can answer each poll once; updating replaces your earlier answers rather
than adding a second set.

## About your comments

Comments aren't shown in the results, and the poll's creator doesn't see them.
Poll System's super administrators can see them when reviewing an account.

## Why can't I answer?

- **"This poll is no longer available for responses."** The poll has closed.
  You can still view its results.
- **"An active subscription is required to participate."** Your membership
  has lapsed: renew from your home page.
- **"Complete your profile."** Add your phone and zipcode.
- **"Submissions disabled by admin for this area."** An admin has turned this
  poll off where you live. Results stay visible.
- **"Poll submissions are temporarily disabled by an administrator."**
  Answering is paused for everyone; try again later.
- **"Please make a selection for…"** or **"Please answer…"**: a question or
  office still needs an answer.
