# Signing in

There are no passwords. Each time you sign in, we email you a **6-digit code**
and a **link**. Use either one; both expire after **15 minutes** and work
**once**.

## On a phone

Your email app opens links in its own built-in browser, not the browser you
started in, so use the code:

1. In your browser, go to the **Sign in** page, type your email, and tap
   **Email me a sign-in link**.
2. The page shows **Check your email**, with a box underneath: **On a phone?
   Type the code from the email.**
3. Switch to your email app and open the BallotBox email. It shows a code such
   as **482 913**. Note the code; you don't need to tap the link.
4. Switch back to your browser. The sign-in page is still there, waiting.
5. Type the code in the box and tap **Sign in**. You're signed in.

## On a computer

1. On the **Sign in** page, type your email and click **Email me a sign-in
   link**.
2. Open the email and click the link. It opens in a new tab, and the sign-in
   page you left open signs you in by itself.

You can type the code instead, as on a phone.

## Just paid for a membership?

After checkout you're back on the home page with **Payment received**. Type
your email and the code from the email there and tap **Sign in**, or click the
link in the email.

## Troubleshooting

- **"That code is wrong or has expired."** Check that you're reading the
  **newest** BallotBox email, then try again. After 5 wrong tries that code
  stops working; ask for a new one with **Wrong address? Try again**, or start
  over on the Sign in page.
- **No email arrived.** Check your spam folder and wait a minute or two.
- **The link signed in the wrong browser.** That's your email app's built-in
  browser. Go back to your own browser and ask for a new code.
