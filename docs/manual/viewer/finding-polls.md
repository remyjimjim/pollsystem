# Finding polls

Open **Search Polls** in the top bar (it's called **View Results** when you're
not signed in). Fill in any of the filters, then press **Search**. Every filter
is optional; filters you fill in all have to match.

## Search by title

Type part of the title in **Title contains**. Capitals don't matter:
`school` finds "School Board Survey" and "New school levy".

## Search by place

Polls cover an area: a zipcode, a county, a state, or the whole country. A
search by place finds every poll whose area **includes** the place you picked.
For example, a statewide poll for Colorado shows up when you search for any
Colorado zipcode, and a nationwide poll shows up for every place.

You can narrow the place in three steps, from widest to narrowest:

1. **State.** Pick one or more states. This alone finds every poll that covers
   any part of those states, and fills in the county and zipcode lists.
2. **County.** Optionally pick counties within those states. Type to find a
   county by name.
3. **Zipcodes.** Optionally pick zipcodes. If you haven't picked a state, you
   can type a single zipcode here directly.

The narrowest choice you make is the one that counts: picking zipcodes searches
just those zipcodes, even if you also picked their county.

The state, county and zipcode lists let you select several at once:

- Click one entry, then **Shift+click** another to select everything between
  them.
- Press **Shift+\*** to select all, and **Shift+0** to clear the selection.

## Other filters

- **Type**: only questionnaires, elections or ballot measures.
- **Candidate name (Election only)**: elections with a candidate whose name
  contains what you typed.
- **See closed polls too**: closed polls are left out unless you tick this.
  They're marked **Closed** in the results.

## The results

Each row shows the poll's title, type, zipcodes and closing date. When a poll
covers many zipcodes, the first few are shown with an **Additional zipcodes**
link for the rest. Click a column heading to sort by it.

- **Vote →** opens the poll so you can answer it. It only appears when you're
  signed in as a member.
- **View results** shows how people have answered so far. Anyone can view
  results.

If nothing matches, you'll see "No active polls match your filters". Try fewer
filters, or tick **See closed polls too**.

## Why can't I find a poll?

- **It's closed.** Tick **See closed polls too**.
- **It doesn't cover the place you searched.** Try the state instead of a
  zipcode, or search by title with no place at all.
- **It isn't published yet.** Polls appear once their creator publishes them.
- **It has been turned off for your area.** Admins can disable a poll in the
  area they look after. It still appears elsewhere.
