# Signing up

You don't need an account to find polls or view results. To **vote and answer
polls**, you need a membership: **$4.99 a month**.

There are no passwords. You sign in with a code or a link sent to your email
(see [Signing in](signing-in.md)).

## Becoming a member

1. Click **Register** in the top bar.
2. Enter your **Email**, **Phone** and **Zipcode**.
   - Use the zipcode where you live: it decides whether your answers count as
     inside or outside a poll's area (see [Viewing results](viewing-results.md)).
   - Each email and each phone number can belong to only one account.
3. Click **Continue to payment** and pay on the secure checkout page.
4. Back on BallotBox, you'll see "Payment received". Check your email: type
   the **6-digit code** from it on that page and tap **Sign in**, or click the
   link in the email. You're signed in.

Your account is created only once payment succeeds. If you cancel checkout,
you aren't charged and nothing is saved, so you can start again with the same
email.

If you're asked to **Complete your profile**, add your phone and zipcode and
click **Save and continue**.

## If your membership lapses

Your membership lapses when your subscription ends: after you cancel, or when
payments keep failing. You can still sign in, find polls and view results, but
you can't vote until you renew.
Your home page shows **Renew** while your membership is lapsed.

## Managing your membership

While you're a member, your home page has:

- **Manage subscription**, to change your payment method.
- **Cancel membership**. After you confirm, your membership stays active until
  the end of the month you've paid for, and isn't renewed.

After cancelling, your home page says when your membership ends, with **Keep my
membership** if you change your mind before then. Once it ends, it lapses (see
above): your answers and account are kept, and **Renew** brings everything
back.

## Deleting your account

Cancelling keeps your account. To have your account and everything in it
deleted for good, including your answers and comments, email us at the address
in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). You'll get an email confirming it's
done.

## Troubleshooting

- **"That email is already registered."** You already have an account: sign in
  instead.
- **"That phone number is already registered to another account."** Each phone
  number can be used for one account only.
- **"We don't have an account for that email."** Check the address for typos,
  or register first.
- **No email arrived, or the code doesn't work.** See
  [Signing in](signing-in.md).
