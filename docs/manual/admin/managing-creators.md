# Managing creators

**Manage Creators** lists everyone with creator access in your area, admins
included. You're in the list too, marked **(you)**. Filter by email with the
box at the top.

| Column    | Shows                                                                                         |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Email     | The creator. **(you)** marks your own row.                                                    |
| Purview   | Their access in your area. Hover or tap the pin for the full list (switched-off access struck through). |
| Polls     | Their enabled polls in your area. The number links to Manage Polls, filtered to them.         |
| Last edit | When they last created, edited, published, archived or restored one of their polls.          |
| Enabled   | Whether they may create polls in your area. Greyed out when you can't change it.              |

## Switching a creator off in your area

Untick **Enabled** to stop someone creating or publishing polls anywhere in your
area. Their existing polls there are disabled too, but only where the poll, the
creator's access and your area overlap; elsewhere they carry on.

You're asked **why**. The reason is optional here, and shows when anyone hovers
over their **No**. Tick **Enabled** again to undo it all.

Re-enabling one of their polls on Manage Polls doesn't re-enable the creator:
they stay switched off until you tick Enabled.

You can't use Enabled on your own row. To pause your own creating, switch off
your own access (below).

## Their access, one area at a time

Click **Edit** on a row to see each piece of access: its area (a state, or
nationwide), its poll types, and whether it's on.

- **Untick** a piece of access to switch it off, and tick it to switch it back
  on. You're asked **why**: optional for someone else, **required for your
  own** access, e.g. "On vacation until Nov 3". Under the access you'll see the
  reason, who switched it off, and when.
- **Remove** deletes access an admin added here. Access that came from an
  approved creator request can only be switched off, not removed, so its
  history is kept.
- **Add access** gives the creator more: choose **whole states** inside your
  area, and optionally which poll types (none ticked = all types).

You can only change access inside your own area. Access elsewhere shows as
**Outside your purview**.

Older access granted for a county or zipcode is still shown and can be
switched off. New access is always for whole states or nationwide.
