# Reviewing creator requests

## Your Admin dashboard

**Admin** in the top bar opens your dashboard. **Reviewing requests for** names
your area, for example "California".

The figures across the top:

- **Pending review**: requests waiting for you.
- **Unassigned in scope**: requests in your area that no admin has picked up,
  with how many are **stale** (waiting more than 48 hours).
- **Creators in scope**: people with creator access in your area, admins
  included.

**Quick links** lead to [Manage Creators](managing-creators.md), [Manage
Polls](managing-polls.md), and **Request more admin coverage** to ask for more
states.

## Which requests you see

When someone asks for creator access, the request goes to an admin of that
area. You see:

- **requests sent to you**;
- **unassigned requests in your area**: ones no admin covered when they were
  sent, or that waited more than two days. They're marked **unassigned**, and
  **stale** after 48 hours;
- **nationwide requests**, which any admin can decide.

## Deciding

Each request shows who asked, the area (whole states or nationwide), the poll
types and their reason.

- On the dashboard, use **✓ Approve** or **✗ Reject** on the request's row.
- **View all** opens **Creator Requests**, where you can tick several and use
  **Approve selected** or **Reject selected**.
- Click a request's **#ID** to open it in full, with **Approve** and
  **Reject**.

The person gets an email either way. When you approve, they become a creator
with access to that area and those poll types.

**Changed your mind?** Open the request and decide again: you can approve a
rejected request, or reject an approved one, which switches that access off.

**Recent Decisions** lists the last 10 requests you decided.
