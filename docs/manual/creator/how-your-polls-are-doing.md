# How your polls are doing

Your Creator dashboard shows how each poll is doing, at a glance.

## Responses

The **Responses** column shows how many people have answered the poll. Each
person counts once, however many questions or candidates they answered. Drafts
show a dash.

Next to it, **% in area** is the share of those people who live inside the
poll's area (its purview). It tells you whether you're reaching the people the
poll is meant for.

Sometimes you'll see **in-area share withheld** instead. That happens while the
people inside the area, or those outside it, number fewer than 10. With so few
people, the split could reveal who answered. The share appears once both groups
are large enough, or one of them is empty.

## Closing dates

The **Close date** column shows when each poll closes. For live polls it also
says how long is left: **closes today**, **closes tomorrow**, or **closes in N
days**. A date that has arrived is highlighted.

## The poll report

**Report** (next to Results) opens a fuller report on a published poll. Only you,
and Poll System's super administrators, can see it.

- **At a glance:** respondents, the share inside the poll's area, how many
  people changed their answers, and how many left a comment (never what they
  wrote).
- **Participation over time:** new respondents per day, with a running total.
  It shows whether sharing the poll on a given day worked.
- **Reach:** respondents in each part of the poll's area (states for a
  nationwide poll, counties for a statewide one, zipcodes for a smaller one),
  people outside the area, and how much of the area you've reached, e.g.
  "Responses from 9 of 14 counties".
- **Highlights** (from the same stepped percentages):
  - elections: each office's leader and margin in percentage points;
  - ballot measures: the margin;
  - questionnaires: questions from most agreed to most divided.
- **Inside vs. outside the area:** the results for people who live in the
  poll's area next to everyone else's.

**Download CSV** saves the report as a spreadsheet file, and **Print** prints it
(or saves it as a PDF) without the site's menus. Both contain exactly what the
page shows, privacy rules included.

## Emails about your poll

You get an email:

- **when your poll reaches 10 responses:** its results have just become
  visible to everyone;
- **when your poll closes:** a short final report, with the number of
  respondents and links to the results and your report.

Each is sent once per poll.

To pause them for a poll, untick its **Emails** box on your Creator dashboard.
Anything that happens while paused isn't emailed later; ticking the box again
only affects future emails. New polls start with emails on.

The report protects people's privacy:

- **Small groups are withheld.** A group of fewer than 10 people shows as
  "fewer than 10". When hiding one group would still let you work it out from
  the total, another group is withheld too.
- **Days are grouped.** Participation over time joins days together until each
  period has at least 10 new respondents.
- **Until a poll has 10 respondents,** the report shows only its totals.

## Results

**Results** opens a published poll's results page, the same page everyone
sees. There you can split the results into people inside and outside the area,
or narrow them by state, county or zipcode. See [Viewing
results](../viewer/viewing-results.md).

What you can't see:

- **Individual answers.** Results are always totals.
- **Comments.** They're private: only Poll System's super administrators can
  see them.
- **Small groups.** Narrowed results for fewer than 10 people are hidden.
- **Early or exact results.** Like everyone, you see results only once 10
  people have responded. They update in steps of 10 and show as percentages,
  never exact counts. See [Viewing results](../viewer/viewing-results.md).
