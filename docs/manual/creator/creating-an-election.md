# Creating an election

An election lists candidates grouped by office (Mayor, Sheriff, and so on).
Voters choose among the candidates for each office. Ballot measures are
attached to an election, so create the election first.

Click **+ New Poll** on your Creator dashboard and choose **Election**.

## Fill in the form

1. **Title**: for example "Springfield municipal election 2026".
2. **Election date**: required.
3. **Purview**: where the election is held. Choose one or more **counties**,
   one or more **states**, or **nationwide**. Elections can't be limited to
   zipcodes. The area must be inside your creator access.
4. **Close date (optional)**: when voting stops. Leave it empty to keep it
   open.
5. **Candidates**: for each one, enter their **Name**, **Affiliation** (party,
   or "Independent") and **Office**. Candidates with the same office are
   grouped together on the ballot, so spell each office the same way every
   time. **+ Add candidate** adds another; **Remove candidate** deletes one. At
   least one complete candidate is required.

## How voters choose

Poll System's super administrators set how voters choose, for all elections:
currently **one candidate per office**. See [Answering
polls](../user/answering-polls.md) for what voters see.

## Save or publish

Click **Save draft** to keep working on it later, or **Publish** to make it
live. See [Drafts, publishing and archiving](drafts-and-publishing.md) for the
checks at publishing and how to revise a published election.

Once the election exists, you can [attach ballot
measures](creating-a-ballot-measure.md) to it.
