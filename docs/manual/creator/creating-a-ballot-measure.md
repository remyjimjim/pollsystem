# Creating a ballot measure

A ballot measure (or referendum) is a single proposal that voters answer **Yes**
or **No**. Every measure belongs to an election, and covers the same area as
that election.

You can attach measures only to elections **you** created. If you haven't
created one yet, [create an election](creating-an-election.md) first.

Click **+ New Poll** on your Creator dashboard and choose **Referendum/Ballot
Measure**.

## Fill in the form

1. **Parent Election**: pick one of your elections from **Select an Election**.
   The measure takes its area from this election, and voters see it as **Part
   of** that election.
2. **Title** and **Summary**: both required. The summary should say what a Yes
   vote and a No vote mean.
3. **Effective date**: required. The date the measure would take effect if
   passed.
4. **Close date (optional)**: when voting stops. Leave it empty to keep it
   open.

## Save or publish

Click **Save draft** to keep working on it later.

**While its election is still a draft,** a measure can't be published on its
own: it goes live **when you publish the election**, so voters never see a
measure before its election. The form says so, and **Back to the election**
takes you there. See [Creating an election](creating-an-election.md).

**Once its election is published,** click **Publish** to make the measure live.

Voters answer measures on their election's page, below the candidates. See
[Drafts, publishing and archiving](drafts-and-publishing.md) for the checks at
publishing and how to revise a published measure.

The election's area must be inside your creator access **for ballot measures**:
access to create elections there isn't enough on its own.
