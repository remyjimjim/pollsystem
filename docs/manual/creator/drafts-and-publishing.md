# Drafts, publishing and archiving

Every poll, whatever its type, goes through the same steps. Start one from
**+ New Poll** on your Creator dashboard (or **Start a new poll** on your home
page), then choose its type:

- [Creating a questionnaire](creating-a-questionnaire.md)
- [Creating an election](creating-an-election.md)
- [Creating a ballot measure](creating-a-ballot-measure.md)

## Your Creator dashboard

**Creator** in the top bar opens your dashboard: every poll you've made, with
its **Type**, **Status** and **Close date**.

| Status    | What it means                                             |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Draft     | Only you can see it. You can still edit it.               |
| Published | Live: people can find it, answer it and see its results.  |
| Archived  | Removed from search, voting and your dashboard's list.    |

## Saving a draft

While filling in a poll, click **Save draft** at any time. The poll stays a
draft until you publish it, and you can come back to it with **Edit** on your
dashboard. Only drafts can be edited.

## Publishing

Click **Publish**. The poll is saved and goes live, and you're taken back to
your dashboard.

The poll is checked when you save a draft, and again when you publish:

- **Its area must be inside your creator access**, for its poll type. If not,
  you'll see "Your creator access doesn't cover…". Choose a smaller area, or
  [ask for more access](../user/becoming-a-creator.md).
- **An admin may have disabled your poll creation** in part of the area. You'll
  see "An admin has disabled your poll creation in part of this area".
- **At publishing, the close date**, if you set one, must be in the future
  the first time you publish. If it's **less than 5 days away**, you're asked
  to confirm: check the date and click **Confirm and publish**, or **Cancel**
  to change it.

## Closing

A poll closes at its **close date**. After that nobody can answer it, but its
results stay visible. A poll without a close date stays open.

## Revising a published poll

Published polls can't be edited directly. To revise one, click **Reopen** on
its row on your dashboard (also on closed polls) and confirm. It goes back to
**draft** and opens for editing. Make your changes, then **Publish** again.

- Until you publish it again, the poll is out of search and voting.
- Its responses are kept. Once a poll has responses, only its title, summary
  and close date can change; its questions, candidates and area stay as they
  were, so earlier answers keep their meaning.
- Reopening an **election** doesn't reopen its ballot measures, but voters
  reach those through the election, so they can't be voted on until you publish
  the election again.

To close a live poll early, reopen it, set its close date to now or earlier,
and publish. You'll be asked to confirm the date.

## History

**History** (on your dashboard, and on each poll's row) lists what happened to
your polls, newest first: published, reopened, closed, archived, restored,
switched off or back on by an admin, and edited by an admin or super. It shows
when, by whom, and details such as what changed when you published again (for
example "Close date: 2026-11-03 18:00 UTC → 2026-11-10 18:00 UTC"). Pick a
poll at the top to see just that poll.

Polls that were already live before history was kept show **Published before
history began**, with no date.

## Archiving

**Archive** takes a poll out of search, voting and your dashboard's list. It
isn't deleted: **Show archived** lists archived polls, and **Restore** brings
one back as a draft.
