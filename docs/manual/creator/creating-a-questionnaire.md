# Creating a questionnaire

A questionnaire is a set of questions that respondents answer **Yes** or
**No**, each with an optional comment.

Click **+ New Poll** on your Creator dashboard and choose **Questionnaire**.

## Fill in the form

1. **Geographic scope**: the area the questionnaire is for (its *purview*).
   Choose a level, then the regions:
   - **Specific zipcodes**
   - **Whole county(ies)**
   - **Whole state(s)**
   - **Nationwide**

   The area must be inside your creator access. Anyone may answer, but
   [results](../viewer/viewing-results.md) can be split into people inside
   and outside this area.
2. **Title** and **Summary**: both required. The title is what people search
   for, so make it specific.
3. **Close date (optional)**: when answering stops. Leave it empty to keep the
   questionnaire open.
4. **Questions**: type each question as a Yes/No question. **+ Add question**
   adds another; **Remove question** deletes one. At least one is required.

## Save or publish

Click **Save draft** to keep working on it later, or **Publish** to make it
live. See [Drafts, publishing and archiving](drafts-and-publishing.md) for the
checks at publishing and how to revise a published questionnaire.

## Tips

- Phrase every question so that Yes and No are clear answers: "Should the
  library open on Sundays?" rather than "What do you think about library
  hours?".
- Keep questions independent. Each is answered and reported on its own.
