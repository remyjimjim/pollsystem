## Statewide ballot measures (exactly three; no SJR/HJR, referendums or advisory votes)

Ballot titles are verbatim from the Attorney General's ballot-title letters on
the SOS site. Check them against the printed voters' pamphlet before
publishing: an appeal over IP26-645's budget statement was pending in August.

1. **Initiative Measure No. IP26-645** (to the People)
   - **Official ballot title:** "concerns state and local taxes. This measure
     would repeal a 9.9% tax on annual individual income over $1,000,000;
     prohibit taxes measured by individual income and taxes on individual
     income or the receipt of individual income; and define "income." This
     measure would decrease funding for public K-12 education, higher education
     (including universities and community colleges), and human services
     (primarily healthcare)."
   - **Source:** https://www.sos.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/IP26-645%20Ballot%20title%20%26%20Summary.pdf
2. **Initiative Measure No. IL26-001** (to the Legislature)
   - **Official ballot title:** "concerns parental rights relating to their
     children in public school. This measure would repeal amendments to a
     statute listing certain rights of parents and guardians of public-school
     children and re-enact the law as it was originally enacted by Initiative
     to the Legislature 2081."
   - **Source:** https://www.sos.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-03/INITIATIVE%20IL26-001%20ballottitleletter.pdf
3. **Initiative Measure No. IL26-638** (to the Legislature)
   - **Official ballot title:** "concerns participation in athletics at K-12
     schools. This measure would prohibit students it defines as "biologically
     male" from competing in certain school athletic activities intended for
     female students only. It would require verification of biological sex by
     students' healthcare providers."
   - **Source:** https://www.sos.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-03/INITIATIVE%20IL26-638%20ballottitleletter.pdf
