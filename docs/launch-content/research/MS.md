# Mississippi: November 3, 2026 general election

Researched 2026-10-23.

## Sources

**Official**
- Mississippi Secretary of State, live Candidate Qualifying List
  (sos.ms.gov/content/CandidateQualifying/default.aspx), headed "All
  offices and candidates currently on file". It was read with curl. All
  names and party labels come from it. The static PDF link for the list
  returned 404.
- Mississippi Legislature all-measures reports for the 2025 and 2026
  regular sessions, plus the SCR 518 history. These were read with curl.

**Secondary (used only to cross-check)**
- Wikipedia's 2026 MS Senate and House pages, for primary results.
- Magnolia Tribune, Oct 13, 2026: the initiative process is still not
  restored.

## Counts

- U.S. Senate: 3 candidates. Cindy Hyde-Smith (R, incumbent), Scott Colom
  (D) and Ty Pinkins (I).
- U.S. House: 4 districts, 12 candidates (3 in each district).
- Measures: none.

## Judicial races

Supreme Court and Court of Appeals seats are elected by district, so none
is statewide and they are left out. The SOS list shows district races for
the Court of Appeals but no Supreme Court race. Any Supreme Court special
election ordered in the Voting Rights Act case would also be
district-based.

## Measures

Every constitutional-amendment resolution in 2025 and 2026 died or was
tabled. One example is SCR 518, the initiative restoration, tabled Feb 11,
2026. The citizen initiative process has not worked since 2021. The rule
for reference is Miss. Const. sec. 273(3): an approved amendment is
inserted by Secretary of State proclamation. The date 2026-11-03 is only
notional.

## Cross-checks

The primary winners on Wikipedia match the SOS list:
- Hyde-Smith 80.8%, Colom 72.9%.
- MS-1: Johnson (D).
- MS-2: Thompson (D) and Eller (R, 51.1%).
- MS-3: Guest and Chiaradio, both unopposed.
- MS-4: Ezell (R) and Hulum (D).

Pinkins left the Democratic primary to run as an Independent.

## Poll close

All of Mississippi is in the Central time zone. Polls are open 7 AM to
7 PM, and 7:00 PM CST is 2026-11-04T01:00Z.

## Gaps

- The SOS list carries no "as of" date. No sample ballot or official
  primary returns were read.
- Party wording on the ballot face was not checked.
- Special sessions were not checked for amendment resolutions. The search
  budget ran out, and Ballotpedia's measures page came back empty.
