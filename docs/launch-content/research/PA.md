## Disagreements

- **Green Party governor ticket (Dastra / Bolton):** Philadelphia's UMOVA notice still lists it. Green Papers says it withdrew on Aug 25. The Erie (8/26), Bucks and Westmoreland documents, WHYY and Spotlight PA all leave it out. It is excluded here.
- **Older county lists:** York (7/31) and Beaver (7/23) predate the Aug 3 deadline for minor-party filings, so they omit Libertarian Krawchuk.
- **PA-7, Andrew Tupone (Green):** removed from the ballot by the courts (Commonwealth Court, upheld by the PA Supreme Court) and now running as a write-in only. Excluded.

## Gaps

- **No Department of State certified list:** the pa.gov candidate pages returned 404, electionreturns.pa.gov is behind bot protection (Incapsula), and Ballotpedia pages came back empty.
- **CD7, CD8, CD13 and CD15 rest on secondary sources only.** Those sources agree with each other. The exact printed name forms are unverified (for example "Glenn Thompson" vs. "G.T. Thompson").
- **Other third-party House candidates:** none were found beyond Dennis Mahoney (The Independent Party, CD3). That is official only for the districts above.
- **No U.S. Senate race, no statewide row offices, and no statewide judicial retention** were found for 2026.
- **No statewide ballot questions.** Local Philadelphia/Allegheny questions are out of scope.

## Times

- **Poll close:** 8:00 PM EST = 2026-11-04T01:00:00Z. The whole state is on Eastern time.
- **Effective date:** none applies because there are no measures. The JSON gives the Art. XI rule and uses Nov 3 as a representative date.
