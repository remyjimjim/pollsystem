# Florida: Nov 3, 2026 general election research

Researched 2026-10-23.

## Official sources (primary)

- **Candidates:** Florida Division of Elections candidate list, 2026 General
  Election, all offices: https://dos.elections.myflorida.com/candidates/CanList.asp
  (the form was posted with `elecid=20261103-GEN`). The list shows the primary
  outcome for each candidate (Won / Eliminated / Unopposed). I kept candidates
  who were "Qualified" and not eliminated, and dropped write-ins (WRI).
- **Party codes:** https://dos.fl.gov/elections/candidates-committees/political-parties/
  (LPF = Libertarian, FFP = Florida Forward, IND = Independent Party of Florida).
- **Amendments:** the DOS initiatives database
  (https://dos.elections.myflorida.com/initiatives/initSearch.asp) has detail pages
  `constitutionalinitiatives.dos.fl.gov/Home/InitDetail?account=10&seqnum=108|109|110`
  that give the ballot number, title and summary for Amendments 1–3.
- **No initiatives:** a DOS press note (Feb 1, 2026) says all 22 citizen
  initiative petitions failed to qualify for 2026.

## Secondary sources (cross-checks only)

- WLRN, Oct 15, 2026: confirms three amendments are on the ballot.
- Florida Phoenix and WGCU (Aug 2026): Amendment 3's original title ("Save Our
  Homes From Excessive Property Taxes") was struck by a judge. The AG then
  rewrote the title and summary, and the DOS page now shows that rewritten text.
- Florida Phoenix voter guide and WUSF/WFSU: match the DOS governor list.
- Ballotpedia News on CD 25: a 2026 mid-decade map moved Moskowitz to CD 25 and
  matches the DOS list.

## Disagreements

- One news report counts six NPA governor tickets. The DOS list has five, and
  I used the DOS list.
- A third-party copy of the DOS list labels primary results "Won/Eliminated".
  These are primary outcomes.

## Decisions and gaps

- **Ballot labels:** Florida ballots print party abbreviations. The JSON uses
  spelled-out labels instead.
- **Names:** I converted names from "Last, First" form. I did not check them
  against a county sample ballot.
- **CD 10:** Frost is unopposed and will not appear on the ballot, so the
  district has no candidates.
- **Amendment 3:** I could not confirm whether anything changed in court after
  mid-August.
- **PDFs:** I could not read the full-text PDFs or the AG letter. The summaries
  came from the HTML database pages.
- **Judicial retention:** Justice Carlos G. Muniz and DCA judges are noted but
  not modeled.
- **Poll close:** 7 pm local. The western Panhandle is on Central time, so the
  last polls close at 7 pm CST, which is 2026-11-04T01:00Z.
- **Effective date:** Art. XI s. 5(e) sets Jan 5, 2027 unless the amendment
  says otherwise. Amendment 3 says Jan 1, 2027.
