# Delaware: November 3, 2026 general election

## Sources
- Delaware Department of Elections, "General Election 11/3/2026 Filed Candidates
  by Office" (last updated 2026-10-22 07:56 PM). This is the main source for every
  candidate and party. Only candidates marked "Qualified" are included.
- Department of Elections general election page: polls are open 7 a.m. until 8 p.m.
  on Election Day, and absentee ballots are due at the 8 p.m. close of polls.
- WHYY treasurer primary guide, used only for background on the open Treasurer seat.

## Contents
- **Statewide (4 offices, 9 candidates):**
  - U.S. Senator: Coons (D), Katz (R)
  - Attorney General: Jennings (D), Graham (R)
  - State Treasurer: Lauzen (D), Ramone (R)
  - Auditor of Accounts: York (D), Callaway (R), Cassidy (Independent Party of Delaware)
- **U.S. House at-large (district 0, 2 candidates):** McBride (D), Arminio (R).
  Republican Lee Murphy withdrew on 7/21/2026 and is left out.
- **Measures:** none. Delaware amends its constitution through two successive
  General Assemblies, with no popular vote, and the official page lists no
  ballot questions.
- **Poll close:** 8:00 PM EST = 2026-11-04T01:00:00Z. The whole state is on Eastern time.

## Confidence
High for the candidates, because the official list was refreshed the day before
this research. The official site lists Cassidy's party as "Ind Pty of DE"; this
file spells it out.

## Gaps
- Lauzen's Democratic primary win is inferred from the general candidate list.
  The official primary results were not read, because the web search budget ran out.
- There is no 2026 sample ballot online yet, so names and party labels on the ballot
  face were not checked against one.
- Ballotpedia's 2026 Delaware measures page could not be read. "No measures"
  rests on the constitutional process and the official page.
