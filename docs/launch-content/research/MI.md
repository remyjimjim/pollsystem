# Michigan: 2026 general election research

Researched 2026-10-23.

## Sources

**Official**
- Michigan Department of State, *Official Candidate Listing, General Election, November 3, 2026* (Bureau of Elections public report, "as of Fri Sep 04 08:48:42 EDT"), linked from https://www.michigan.gov/sos/elections/election-results-and-data:

  Source for all candidates and parties.
- Board of State Canvassers page, https://www.michigan.gov/sos/elections/bsc, with its agendas for 2026-08-17 (the draft Proposal 26-2 language) and 2026-08-24 (the "26-N" numbering). The PDFs were read with a small zlib text extractor.

**Secondary (used for the proposal wording and to cross-check)**
- ClickOnDetroit's Wayne and Oakland county proposal lists (the text used here), and FOX 2 Detroit's Prop 1 and Prop 2 explainers, which cite the BSC.
- Michigan Public, 2026-08-17 and 2026-09-04; Bridge Michigan (the citizenship amendment was kept off the ballot); Citizens Research Council (CRC), for the "Proposal 26-1/26-2" names.

## Results
- **9 statewide offices:** U.S. Senate, Governor/Lt. Governor, Secretary of State, Attorney General, State Board of Education, UM Regents, MSU Trustees, WSU Governors (vote for 2 on each board), and Supreme Court (2 seats, nonpartisan).
- **13 House districts,** with 62 candidates on the ballot.
- **2 measures:** 26-1 (constitutional convention, required every 16 years) and 26-2 (an initiated law on utility and contractor contributions and political ad disclosure).
- **Polls:** the latest close is 8 PM CST in the four Central-time Upper Peninsula counties, which is 02:00 UTC on Nov 4.

## Disagreements
- **Prop 26-1:** ClickOnDetroit capitalizes "State Constitution" in the question; FOX 2 uses lowercase.
- **Designation:** the BSC 2026-08-17 draft and ClickOnDetroit say "Proposal 2026-2"; the BSC 2026-08-24 agenda and CRC use "26-2". The JSON uses "26-N".
- **Prop 26-2 caption:** the BSC draft lacks "and government contractors". All news sources say the Board added it on Aug 17, and the published ballot text includes it.

## Gaps
- **Final proposal wording:** no official page has it. michigan.gov has no 2026 proposals page, and the Aug 17 minutes are not posted. The 26-2 bullets and question match the official draft word for word. All of 26-1's wording comes from news sources.
- **Candidate listing date:** 2026-09-04. The live MVIC listing is behind a bot check, so later changes are unverified.
- **Disqualified candidates:** those marked DISQ were dropped: one in MI-7 and three in MI-13.
- **Effective date:** 2026-12-08 is an estimate. The rule is 10 days after the canvass is certified, and the date assumes certification around Nov 28.
