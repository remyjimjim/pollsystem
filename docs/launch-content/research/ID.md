# Idaho: 2026 general election research

Researched 2026-10-23 for the November 3, 2026 election.

## Sources

**Official (primary)**

- Idaho SOS candidate portal (run.voteidaho.gov, "Search Filed Candidates"). Read through its public JSON API
  (`api-run.voteidaho.gov/api/FiledCandidates/SearchCandidates`) for election "11/03/2026 - 2026 GENERAL". The
  portal marks the list final (592 filings).
- VoteIdaho general-election page (https://voteidaho.gov/idaho-general-election/): HJR 4 and HJR 6 ballot
  questions, Proposition 1 titles, the advisory question, and poll hours (8 a.m. to 8 p.m.).
- VoteIdaho initiatives page, the Legislature's 2026 amendments page, and Idaho Code 34-1211 (state canvass
  within 21 days) and 34-1809 (ballot titles).

**Secondary (cross-check only)**

- The Green Papers lists the same candidates, plus a write-in and a withdrawn candidate that are excluded here.

## Findings

- U.S. Senate: 4 candidates. There is no Democrat: David Roth withdrew on Sep 1, and Todd Achilles runs as an
  Independent.
- Seven state offices: Governor (5 candidates), Superintendent (3), and Lt. Governor, Secretary of State,
  Controller, Treasurer and Attorney General (2 each).
- U.S. House: CD-1 has 4 candidates and CD-2 has 5.
- The Constitution Party's nominee for governor is on the list under the ballot name "Pro-Life".
- Measures:
  - HJR 4: only the Legislature may legalize marijuana or other psychoactive substances.
  - HJR 6: English becomes the official language in the constitution.
  - Proposition 1: Reproductive Freedom and Privacy Act, an initiated statute.
- Advisory question (HB 932): a non-binding question asking voters to choose a state gun from six options. It is
  multiple-choice rather than yes/no, so it is recorded in the notes and not in `measures`.
- Effective date: the amendments take effect when the state canvass certifies them, by Nov 24, 2026 (the
  representative date). Proposition 1 would take effect Jan 1, 2027.
- Polls close at 8:00 PM local time. The latest closing is in the Pacific-time panhandle, at 8:00 PM PST, which is
  2026-11-04T04:00:00Z.

## Gaps

- Names come from the filing portal and were not checked against a printed sample ballot. Some lack periods
  (for example "Brandon D Woolf").
- It is unclear whether Proposition 1's short or long ballot title prints on the ballot. The short title is used.
- The web-search budget ran out, so the Roth withdrawal was not confirmed in news.
- Primary results were not reviewed separately. The final general-election list governs.
