# South Carolina: 2026 general election research

Researched 2026-10-23 for the November 3, 2026 election.

## Sources

**Official (primary)**

- SC Election Commission Candidate Tracking System (VREMS), 11/3/2026 Statewide General Election (election ID 22596):
  https://vrems.scvotes.sc.gov/Candidate/CandidateSearch?electionId=22596. Queried per office, using the same
  form POST the page makes. Only candidates with status "Active" are included.
- VREMS Referendum Search for the same election:
  https://vrems.scvotes.sc.gov/Candidate/ReferendumSearch?electionId=22596. It returned 42 local questions in
  23 counties. The "Statewide Constitutional Amendments" and "Advisory Questions" filters returned nothing.
- scvotes.gov 2026 prep page, for poll hours (7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.): https://scvotes.gov/voters/2026prep/

**Secondary (cross-check only)**

- AP via OPB (Aug 26, 2026): Lindsey Graham died July 11. Darline Graham was appointed to the seat, then won the
  Aug 11 special GOP primary and the Aug 25 runoff over Ralph Norman. This matches VREMS, which lists the
  Aug 11 "US Senate Special Republican Primary" and gives Lindsey Graham's status as "Deceased After Primary".
- politics1.com/sc.htm: its candidate list matches VREMS except for the points below.
- Ballotpedia pages came back empty through WebFetch and could not be used.

## Findings

- Statewide: U.S. Senate (4 candidates), Governor and Lieutenant Governor (4 tickets), Secretary of State (2),
  Attorney General (2), Treasurer (2), Comptroller General (2), Superintendent of Education (3) and
  Commissioner of Agriculture (4). Adjutant General has not been an elected office since 2019.
- U.S. House: 7 districts, 18 candidates.
- No fusion candidacies: each candidate appears under only one party.
- Measures: none statewide. Under S.C. Const. Art. XVI §1, an amendment approved by voters still needs
  ratification by the General Assembly at its next session. 2027-01-12, the day that session convenes, is used
  as the representative date.
- Polls close at 7:00 PM EST, which is 2026-11-04T00:00Z. The whole state is on Eastern time.

## Disagreements and gaps

- SC-2: VREMS lists Dayna Alane Smith as "Workers", while politics1 lists her as UC. The official label is used.
- Superintendent: politics1 names the UC candidate as Steven "Baba Amin Ojuok" Richmond. The VREMS ballot name,
  "Baba Amin Ojuok", is used.
- VREMS names have no punctuation (e.g. "James E Jim Clyburn"). They were not checked against a printed sample
  ballot, because those require a voter login.
- That there are no statewide questions rests on VREMS alone. No secondary source could confirm it.
