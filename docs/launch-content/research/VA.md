# Virginia: 2026 general election research

Researched 2026-10-23 for the November 3, 2026 election.

## Sources

**Official (primary)**

- Virginia Department of Elections (ELECT), *November 3, 2026 - Federal Offices* candidate list (HTML table; file rev. 10-6-2026):
  https://www.elections.virginia.gov/casting-a-ballot/candidate-list/november-3-2026-gen-elect-federal-offices/
- ELECT, *November 2026 Proposed Constitutional Amendments and Local Referenda* (HTML; rev. 9-8-2026), giving the ballot question text verbatim.
- ELECT voter brochure for the three amendments (PDF). It has the questions, the full text and the explanations.
- ELECT, *2026 Changes to Virginia's Election Laws* (PDF). It gives the effective dates and confirms the redistricting nullification.
- LIS, 2026 Acts ch. 1 (the marriage referendum act). It says an approved amendment "shall become effective on January 1, 2027."
- ELECT, *Casting a Ballot* page: polls are open 6:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.

WebFetch could not read the PDFs. Their text was extracted locally with a stdlib Python decoder that maps glyphs through ToUnicode, and the output read back cleanly. The LIS bill pages for HB781 and HB963 were unreadable.

**Secondary (cross-check only)**

- WMRA/WHRO primary results (Aug 4, 2026): Mizusawa, Luria, Perriello, Taylor and Ollivant won their primaries, which matches ELECT.
- Virginia Mercury, Jurist and Wikipedia, on redistricting.

## Findings

- Statewide: U.S. Senate only (Warner D, Mizusawa R).
- U.S. House: 11 districts with 36 candidates, 2 to 6 per district.
- Redistricting: voters approved a mid-decade amendment on April 21, 2026, but the Supreme Court of Virginia nullified it and SCOTUS declined to step in. **The 2021 map stays in effect.**
- Measures: Questions 1–3 cover reproductive freedom, marriage, and the right to vote / restoration of voting rights. All three take effect **January 1, 2027** if approved.
- Polls close at 7:00 PM EST, which is 2026-11-04T00:00:00Z.

## Disagreements

- Secondary sources give the date of the state Supreme Court's redistricting ruling as either April 28 or May 8, 2026. The map in use is the same either way.
- Otherwise, official and secondary sources agree.

## Gaps

- The party labels printed on the ballot face were not checked against a sample ballot.
- The result of the Tazewell suit over the wording of Question 1 was not found. ELECT still publishes the original wording, and early voting began Sept 18.
- The Jan 1, 2027 effective date for Questions 1 and 3 comes from ELECT's summary, not from the enabling acts.
- Candidate changes after Oct 6 were not checked.
