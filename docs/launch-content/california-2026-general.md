# California, November 3, 2026 general election: research for launch polls

Researched 2026-10-23, from official sources only.

**Sources:**

- **Candidates, party preferences and designations:** the Secretary of State's
  Certified List of Candidates (signed 8/27/2026),
  https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-general/cert-list-candidates.pdf.
  The 42-page PDF was read page by page. Spot-checked: CA-40 Calvert vs Kim,
  Governor Becerra vs Hilton, CA-11 Chan vs Wiener, and no U.S. Senate
  contest.
- **Propositions:** the official Voter Information Guide,
  https://voterguide.sos.ca.gov/propositions/N/. The count (14) is confirmed
  by https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/ballot-measures/qualified-ballot-measures.

The contests and their candidates are in `scripts/launch-content/ca-2026-statewide.json`
and `ca-2026-house.json`.

## What is on the ballot

- **U.S. Senate:** no contest.
- **Statewide offices (12):**
  - Governor
  - Lieutenant Governor
  - Secretary of State
  - Controller
  - Treasurer
  - Attorney General
  - Insurance Commissioner (Democrat vs Democrat)
  - Superintendent of Public Instruction (nonpartisan)
  - Board of Equalization, districts 1–4 (districts 2 and 3 are Democrat vs
    Democrat)
- **Judicial retention:** yes/no votes on Supreme Court Associate Justices
  Kelli M. Evans and Joshua Groban, plus Court of Appeal retentions. Not
  polled: they are retention questions, not candidate races.
- **U.S. House (52, top-two):**
  - Eight districts are Democrat vs Democrat: 4, 7, 11, 12, 14, 29, 34, 37.
  - One is Republican vs Republican: 40, Ken Calvert vs Young Kim.
  - Kevin Kiley (CA-6) is listed as No Party Preference.
  - The certified list doesn't mark House incumbents, so none are marked.
- **Propositions (14):** 1–5 and 37–45; there are no Props 6–36. Titles are
  official. Each summary paraphrases the official summary in the guide.

## Notes

- **Prop 43:** its title is "Limits Voters' Ability…". The guide's index page
  has a typo ("Limit's"); the proposition's own page was checked.
- **"Herb W Morgan"** (Controller) is spelled as extracted from the PDF, with
  no period after the W.
- **Effective date: 2026-12-16 is approximate.** Measures generally take
  effect 5 days after the Secretary of State certifies the vote, which is due
  Dec 11. Some measures set their own dates.
- **Polls close** at 8 pm Pacific (2026-11-04T04:00Z).
