# Colorado, November 3, 2026 general election: research for launch polls

Researched 2026-10-23, official sources first.

**Sources:**

- **Candidates and parties:** the Secretary of State's 2026 General Election
  Official Candidate List,
  https://www.coloradosos.gov/pubs/elections/vote/generalCandidates.html. It is
  "certified to the counties on September 4" and was updated September 15.
  Write-ins are left out (they aren't printed on the ballot).
- **Incumbents:** senate.gov and clerk.house.gov.
- **Measures:** the Secretary of State's 2026 measures page,
  https://www.coloradosos.gov/pubs/elections/Initiatives/ballot/contacts/2026.html,
  with the official ballot titles verbatim. The count (14) matches the
  Legislative Council's "fourteen statewide measures". Not checked against the
  Blue Book PDF, which couldn't be read here.

The contests and their candidates are in `scripts/launch-content/co-2026-statewide.json`
and `co-2026-house.json`.

## What is on the ballot

- **U.S. Senate:** the Class II seat. John Hickenlooper (incumbent) and 5
  others.
- **Statewide offices:**
  - Governor/Lieutenant Governor (7 tickets; open seat)
  - Secretary of State (5)
  - State Treasurer (4)
  - Attorney General (2)
- **U.S. House (8):** every candidate printed on the ballot.
  - CO-1 is open: Diana DeGette lost the June 30 primary to Melat Kiros
    (Colorado Politics, 2026-07-01). The certified list agrees.
- **Measures (14):** Amendments 81–87 (each needs 55%), Proposition NN
  (referred by the legislature), and Propositions 132–137.
  - Amendment 87's official title refers to a table of estimated tax changes,
    which isn't reproduced here; the poll summary points to the source.
- **Left out:**
  - District races: CU Regent districts 2, 6, 7 and State Board of Education
    districts 1, 3, 7.
  - Regional Ballot Issue 7A (Front Range Passenger Rail, 13 counties).
  - Judicial retention questions.

## Notes

- **Secretary of State race:** Ballotpedia still shows Alex Astley as the
  Libertarian. The official list has Sean Vadney, who replaced Astley through
  a vacancy committee.
- **Withdrawals after September 15** wouldn't show on the list.
- **Effective date: 2026-12-31 is approximate.** Measures take effect on the
  governor's proclamation, within 30 days of the official canvass.
- **Polls close** at 7 pm Mountain (2026-11-04T02:00Z).
