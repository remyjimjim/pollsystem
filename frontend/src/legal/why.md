# Why BallotBox?

## Anonymous

Other members never see your email address or phone number. You see your own
only when you're signed in. We suggest an email address that uses a pseudonym
or a place; there are many free providers, such as Yahoo and Proton. Public
results and public comments never show who said what. Our staff can see account
details when they need to, for support and moderation; see the
[Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

Everything travels encrypted between you and BallotBox, and our hosting
providers encrypt it where it's stored.

Speak your mind: public comments are shown without your name. We only hold
back slurs and profanity.

## Hear from your neighbors

Target specific zipcodes to get the opinions of your neighbors. Create a town
hall questionnaire, or put a referendum to your neighbors and see how they feel
about it.

## Chat (coming soon)

Pick a unique nickname, add it to your profile, and start chatting.

## The more members, the more it means

Results reflect the members who choose to answer. The more people join, the
closer the results come to a whole community, and with enough members,
election results could even become statistically meaningful.

## A record of your own opinions 

Look back at every ballot and questionnaire you've answered, and print them or send a screenshot of your ballot to an interested party.

Keep your finger on the pulse of your community and remember where you stood.
It's a win-win.
