# Anonymous

Users emails and phone numbers aren't shown ever in BallotBox except your own to you when you're logged in and they're encrypted over the wire and in storage.  Say what you feel all day long.

## Target specific zipcodes to get the opinions of your neighbors

Create for instance town hall questionnaires.  Create referenda and see how your neighbors feel about them.  

## Chat coming soon

Pick a unique nickname, add it to your profile and start chatting.

## If enough users join then election results could reach statistical significance

## Historical record of voting

You can print out any of your paste completed ballots.  

Keep your finger on the pulse of your community and recall your own opinions.  It's a win win.
