# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [[TO FILL: date this policy takes effect]]

## What we collect

- **Your account:** your email address, phone number and zipcode.
- **Your membership:** the identifiers our payment processor (Stripe) gives
  your customer record and subscription, and the date your paid membership
  runs to. **We never see or store your card details**: Stripe handles payment
  directly.
- **What you do on the service:**
  - your answers to polls;
  - any comments you add, and whether you chose to make each one public;
  - requests for creator or admin access, including the reason you give;
  - polls you create, and a history of what happened to them (published,
    reopened, closed, and so on, with who did it and when).
- **Emails we send you:** sign-in links, and for poll creators, updates about
  their polls. Our email provider processes these to deliver them.

We **don't** use advertising trackers or cookies. Your browser keeps four small
items for the service to work:

- your sign-in token;
- the id of the account last signed in on that browser;
- your language choice;
- which columns you chose to show on the poll search page.

## How we use it

- To run the service: sign you in, keep your membership active, and let you
  answer polls.
- To match you with polls for your area: your zipcode places you inside or
  outside a poll's area.
- To keep results private and trustworthy (below).
- To prevent abuse, for example by limiting how often sign-in links can be
  requested.

We don't sell your information.

## Who sees what

- **The public** sees only **combined results**, as rounded percentages, and
  only once a poll has a minimum number of respondents (currently 4). Results
  then update in steps of that size. Groups smaller than that minimum are never
  shown on their own.
- **Public comments** are shown without your name, under the same rules.
  Comments are private unless you make them public.
- **Poll creators** see combined results and a report about their own polls:
  totals, never one person's answers.
- **Admins** see combined results for polls in their area, and the tools to
  moderate them.
- **Super administrators**, the service's staff, can see an individual member's
  account details, answers and comments, for support and moderation.

## Who processes it for us

- **Stripe:** payments and subscriptions.
- **Resend:** sending email.
- **Fly.io:** running the service's servers (United States).
- **Neon:** the database (Amazon Web Services, US East).
- **Cloudflare:** delivering the website.

Each handles your information only to provide its service to us.

## Your choices

- **Comments:** they stay private unless you tick "Make comment public".
- **Emails:** poll creators can pause updates about each poll.
- **Cancelling** your membership keeps your account and answers, so you can
  renew later.
- **Deleting your account:** email [[TO FILL: contact email]] from your
  account's email address. We delete your account, answers and comments, and
  cancel any subscription at once. Results of polls you answered are recounted
  without you. Our payment processor keeps its own payment records, as the law
  requires.
- **Correcting** your details: email us the same way.
